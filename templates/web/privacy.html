Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

This policy explains what {{ site_name }} ("we", "the app") collects, why, and what you can do about it. We don't sell your data, show ads, or use third-party analytics or advertising trackers.

What we collect

  • Account details - your name, email address, and a securely hashed password (we never store or see your actual password).
  • Places you save - each place's name, coordinates, address, category, your notes, and settings like favorite, visited, and nearby alerts. If you saved it from Instagram, the link to that reel.
  • Reel links you share to the app - so we can find the places a reel mentions.

Your location

With your permission, the app uses your device's location to show you on the map, sort search results by distance, and - only if you turn on nearby alerts - notice when you're close to a saved place. Nearby alerts are checked on your device; your ongoing location is not sent to our servers. When you search for a place, an approximate location (rounded to about a kilometer) may be sent with the search so results near you come first.

Services we use to run the app

  • Instagram - when you share a reel, our server fetches that public reel's caption.
  • Google Gemini - the caption is sent to Google's Gemini API to identify the places it mentions. It is not linked to your account when sent.
  • Photon (geocoding) - place names you search for, and places found in reels, are looked up on a geocoding service to get their map coordinates.
  • OpenFreeMap (map tiles) - the map images are loaded from OpenFreeMap, which, like any website, sees your device's IP address when serving them.
  • Email delivery - to send password reset emails.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and saved places until you delete them. Deleting a place removes it immediately. Deleting your account permanently removes your account and every place you saved. Captions and places we find for a public reel are cached for up to 30 days so repeat shares are fast; this cache isn't linked to any account.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in the app (Profile → Delete account) or on the web at {{ public_base_url }}/delete-account/.

Security

Connections to our servers are encrypted, passwords are hashed, and sign-in sessions can be revoked by changing your password (which signs out every other device).

Children

{{ site_name }} is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above, and for significant changes we'll let you know in the app.
